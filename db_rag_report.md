## Legal Analysis (Database-Backed)

### Case Summary
ABC Traders (Pvt) Ltd (incorporated 2018) has committed multiple violations under The Companies Act 2017:
1. Failed to file annual returns for 2021-2023
2. Failed to maintain proper books of accounts
3. Failed to hold AGMs for 3 consecutive years
4. Failed to update register of members
5. Ignored SECP compliance notices

### Violations (with DB sources)
1. **Annual Return Filing Default**  
   - **Violation:** Section 130(3) requires annual returns to be filed within 30 days of AGM/calendar year-end.  
   - **Penalty:** Section 130(6)(b) imposes Level 1 penalties for non-listed companies.  

2. **Books of Accounts Maintenance Failure**  
   - **Violation:** Section 220(1) mandates maintenance of books showing true financial position.  
   - **Penalty:** Section 401(1) prescribes imprisonment up to 3 years or fine up to PKR 100,000 for officers in default during winding-up.  

3. **AGM Non-Compliance**  
   - **Violation:** Section 132(1) requires AGMs within 120 days of financial year-end.  
   - **Penalty:** Section 132(5)(b) imposes Level 1 penalties for private companies.  

4. **Register of Members Default**  
   - **Violation:** Section 119(1) requires updated member registers with particulars.  
   - **Penalty:** Section 119(4) imposes Level 1 penalties.  

5. **SECP Notice Ignorance**  
   - **Violation:** Implied non-compliance under Sections 130/132/220/119 after formal notices.  
   - **Penalty:** Aggravated penalties under Section 252 for persistent non-compliance.  

### Analysis
- **Systemic Non-Compliance:** The company ignored statutory filing deadlines (Sections 130, 132), record-keeping (Sections 119, 220), and regulatory engagement.  
- **Aggravating Factors:** Three consecutive years of violations and disregard for SECP notices demonstrate willful neglect.  
- **Liability Focus:** Directors/officers face personal liability under Sections 220(6) and 401(1) for accounts/AGM defaults.  

### Penalties
1. **Monetary Fines:**  
   - Level 1 penalties (PKR 100,000+ per violation) under Sections 130(6), 132(5), 119(4).  
2. **Director Liability:**  
   - Imprisonment up to 3 years (Section 401) for accounts violations.  
3. **Compounding Fees:**  
   Additional penalties under Section 452 for delayed compliance.  

### Recommendations
1. **Immediate Compliance:**  
   - File overdue returns (Section 130)  
   - Hold belated AGMs (Section 132)  
   - Update member register (Section 119)  
2. **Internal Audit:**  
   Conduct forensic audit of books under Section 221(1).  
3. **SECP Settlement:**  
   Apply for penalty compounding under Section 452 within 30 days.  
4. **Governance Overhaul:**  
   Appoint compliance officer and implement Sections 220/119 record-keeping protocols.
